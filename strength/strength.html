Strength Training

Strength training, also known as resistance training, helps you build muscle strength and endurance. You can use free weights, resistance bands, or even your own body weight to perform these exercises.

Benefits of Strength Training

  • Increases muscle mass and bone density
  • Improves posture and flexibility
  • Boosts metabolism and burns calories
  • Reduces stress and promotes better sleep

Tips for Beginners

Start slow, focus on your form, and rest between sessions. Remember that consistency is the key to seeing results. Don’t forget to warm up before and stretch after your workout.
